# Provenance

The `physics/` files are a byte-identical snapshot of this research project's
existing PyTorch implementation, not a redistribution of the official JAX repository.
`SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records their original relative paths and SHA-256 hashes.

Scientific reference: **Wasserstein Residuals: Learning Gradient Flows from Population Dynamics**;
method name **Stitching**. Official implementation and its license:
https://github.com/BasisResearch/wasserstein-residuals

The independent local-growth model and auxiliary OT/Sinkhorn/rollout terms are
project extensions. This code release makes no claim to reproduce the exact
official balanced training experiment or to identify measured cell lineages.
Dependencies retain their respective licenses. No project-wide publication
license is assigned here on the owner's behalf.
